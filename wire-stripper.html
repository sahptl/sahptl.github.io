Automated wire stripper

A bench-top machine that feeds wire off a spool in 0.4 mm increments and closes two hand strippers through stepper-driven linkages.

When
Summer 2026
Where
University mechatronics lab

From concept sketch to build

The first idea was a strip-then-cut line with three powered feed roller stations and a servo-driven linkage on each tool. The build kept the linkage-driven tools but switched to stepper motors and a single feed station.

How it works

A stepper drives a pair of rubber-banded pinch rollers that pull wire off the spool and push it through guide tubes to the strippers. Two more steppers on a printed frame drive the linkages that close the stripper handles. I sized the motors and drivers from a torque-speed analysis.

Electronics and firmware

An ATmega328 runs three A4988 stepper drivers, one per motor, from a 12 V supply. A potentiometer and pushbutton set the cutting parameters, and a 16x2 LCD shows the active settings and progress of each run. The firmware is register-level AVR C.
